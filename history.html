Tower Medic Pharmacy legacy

60+ Years of Personal Care.

Family-owned, Fort Worth-rooted, and committed to personalized pharmaceutical care since 1964.

1964

Founded in Fort Worth

Tower Medic Pharmacy began as part of the Moreland Drugs family, providing personal pharmaceutical care and building trusted community relationships.

1973

A Family-Owned Pharmacy

Melvin and Leisa Musgrove purchased Tower Medic Pharmacy, beginning a new chapter centered on service, clinical expertise, and individualized care.

Six decades

Growing With Our Community

Tower Medic Pharmacy expanded from a traditional neighborhood pharmacy into a healthcare partner serving patients, prescribers, hospitals, and long-term care organizations across Texas and beyond.

2022

The Next Generation

Chris Musgrove assumed ownership, continuing the family's commitment to compassionate care while investing in technology and expanded clinical capabilities.

Today

Personalized Care, Advanced Capabilities

Our team prepares sterile and non-sterile compounded medications, supported by experienced professionals, controlled processes, and in-house analytical testing.
